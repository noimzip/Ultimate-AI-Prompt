[Objective and Theoretical Background]
From a vast amount of learning materials, extract knowledge gaps using multiple axes—"Importance," "Difficulty," and "Probability of appearing in exams"—to construct a study roadmap. This process applies the structure of the "Gap Hunter" prompt used in academic literature reviews. Instead of simply extracting keywords, it forces the LLM to look for "theoretical/empirical gaps" and "Contradictions between sources," thereby analyzing deep semantic relationships across the entire context. Furthermore, by applying pedagogical weighting based on Cognitive Load Theory, it accurately identifies the "Threshold concepts" where learners are most likely to stumble.

[Task Definition: Recursive Algorithmic Analysis of Study Priorities]
Read through all uploaded materials carefully and generate a complete roadmap determining the "study priorities" for exam preparation. You must strictly pass through the following internal Phases.

Phase 1: Exhaustive Extraction of Concepts and Application of Gap Hunter Algorithm
Extract all major concepts, terms, formulas, and theories from all sources without omission (MECE principle). Then, thoroughly identify "logical contradictions," "areas lacking information (Unknown unknowns)," and "important areas heavily emphasized across multiple documents." If contradictions exist, analyze why they occur (differences in prerequisites or context).

Phase 2: Calculation of Pedagogical Weights and Prioritization
For each concept, calculate a score from 0 to 100 based on the following three criteria to determine priority:
- Predictability Score for Exams (inferred from mention frequency, heading hierarchy, and degree of emphasis in materials)
- Cognitive Load Score (complexity of the concept, necessity of prerequisite knowledge, and level of abstraction)
- Knowledge Bottleneck Degree (the extent to which failing to understand this concept fatally hinders the understanding of other concepts)

Phase 3: Recursive Self-Improvement Loop
After creating the initial priority list, automatically execute a Self-Critique. Ask yourself: "Is there bias in the importance evaluation?", "Are there subtle footnotes or charts I missed?", "Has guesswork from internal knowledge crept in?" Identify and correct at least three weaknesses before generating the final version.

- Study Priority Matrix (Markdown Table): Concept Name, Overall Priority (S, A, B, C), the 3 scores determined in Phase 2, relevant source file names (citations), and detailed reasoning for why that priority was assigned.
